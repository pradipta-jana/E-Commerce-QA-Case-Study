# Product Discovery Test Scenarios

**Requirement Group:** Product Discovery  
**Jira:** To be linked

### TS-PROD-11 — Product Listing

Verify that customers can access the Products page and view available products.

### TS-PROD-12 — Successful Product Search

Verify that a valid product can be searched successfully and is displayed in the search results.

### TS-PROD-13 — Product Details

Verify that a customer can open a selected product and view its details and price.

### TS-PROD-14 — Product Category Browsing

Verify that products can be browsed using an available category.

### TS-PROD-15 — Product Brand Browsing

Verify that products can be browsed using an available brand.
