# Customer Login Test Scenarios

**Requirement:** REQ-CAM-03 — Customer Login  
**Jira:** [KAN-9](https://pradipta-qa-portfolio.atlassian.net/browse/KAN-9)

### TS-CAM-07 — Successful Customer Login

Verify that a registered customer can log in successfully using valid credentials.

### TS-CAM-08 — Invalid Email

Verify that login is prevented when an invalid or unregistered email address is provided.

### TS-CAM-09 — Invalid Password

Verify that login is prevented when an incorrect password is provided.

### TS-CAM-10 — Missing Login Credentials

Verify that the login process handles missing email or password appropriately.
