# Shopping Cart Test Scenarios

**Requirement Group:** Shopping Cart  
**Jira:** To be linked

### TS-CART-16 — Add Product to Cart

Verify that a selected product can be added to the shopping cart.

### TS-CART-17 — Add Multiple Products

Verify that multiple configured products can be added to the cart.

### TS-CART-18 — Add Product with Required Quantity

Verify that a product can be added with the required quantity.

### TS-CART-19 — Verify Product Price

Verify that the product price displayed in the cart matches the selected product price.

### TS-CART-20 — Verify Cart Total

Verify that the cart total is calculated correctly using product price and quantity.

### TS-CART-21 — Remove Product

Verify that a product can be removed from the shopping cart.

### TS-CART-22 — Verify Empty Cart

Verify that the appropriate empty-cart state is displayed after all products are removed.
