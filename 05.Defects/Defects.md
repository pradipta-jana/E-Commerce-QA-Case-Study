# Defects & Observations

## Defect Summary

No functional defects were identified within the **currently executed test scope**.

The selected happy-path scenarios covering authentication, product search, product details, shopping cart, checkout, order placement, and order confirmation were executed successfully.

The project does not aim to provide complete application coverage. Therefore, the absence of reported defects should not be interpreted as confirmation that the application is defect-free.

Additional negative, boundary, validation, and exploratory testing may identify defects outside the current scope.
